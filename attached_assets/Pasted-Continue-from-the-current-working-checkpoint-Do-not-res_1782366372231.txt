The system is now running and the main pages are working. I want you to add and polish an important real-world business workflow for Egyptian sanitary ware / paints / plumbing shops:

Craftsman-to-Customer invoice linking.

Business meaning:
In this type of shop, a craftsman/plumber/painter may come to the shop and buy items on behalf of a specific customer. I may already have the craftsman saved in the Craftsmen page, and I may also have the customer saved in the Customers page. When creating a quotation or sales invoice, I need to link both:

* The craftsman who brought or handled the purchase
* The actual customer who owns the job/invoice

Example:
I have a craftsman called “أحمد السباك”.
He brings a customer called “محمد علي”.
The customer takes an invoice.
I want the invoice to show that it belongs to customer “محمد علي” and was brought/handled by craftsman “أحمد السباك”.

This must be implemented as real database logic, not just UI.
